# ScreenForge Privacy Policy

ScreenForge is a free menu-bar app that takes screenshots on your Mac and lets you annotate them.

## Polski

ScreenForge to darmowa aplikacja do zrzutów ekranu na Macu. Zrzuty, projekty i historia zostają tylko na tym komputerze. Aplikacja nie wysyła obrazów, nie wymaga konta i nie zawiera analityki. Uprawnienie do nagrywania ekranu służy wyłącznie do zrobienia zrzutu. Buy Me a Coffee otwiera się w przeglądarce i jest opcjonalne.

## What stays on your Mac

Screenshots, annotation projects, and capture history are stored only on this Mac, in the app's sandbox and in the Pictures folder when you save a file. ScreenForge does not upload images, does not require an account, and does not include analytics or advertising.

## Screen Recording

macOS asks for Screen Recording permission so ScreenForge can capture your screen. That permission is used only when you take a screenshot. The image is not sent anywhere.

## Optional support

Buy Me a Coffee opens in your browser. ScreenForge does not receive your payment details. That link is optional.
